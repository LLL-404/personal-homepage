## The Return of Serifs

For the past decade, sans-serif fonts dominated web design. But recently, serifs are making a comeback.

### Why?

- Serifs offer better readability at small sizes
- They bring an "editorial" weight to content
- On high-DPI screens, serif details shine

## Principles

1. **Typography as visual**: Let fonts be the design
2. **Restrained color**: Black, white, gray, one accent
3. **Space as rhythm**: Use whitespace to pace reading
